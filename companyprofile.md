About Company
KENFAB ALUMINIUM AND INTERIORS was founded in 2019 as an
Aluminium, Interiors, and light Steel Fabrication company. We have since
become a reputable player in transforming the Kenyan industry. Our executive
team is led by the 3 founders, whose wealth of experience in their diverse
roles in the construction industry has driven the company’s exponential
growth to its current successful levels.
This experience covers the design, implementation, and project management
in Building and construction. We provide BESPOKE solutions tailored to each
of our customers. This allows us to service a wide variety of customers from
small to major enterprises.
KENFAB has the necessary resources, capacity, and experience to meet
current and future customer requirements. Management at KENFAB is
confident that the company will continue its successful path far and beyond.
The company’s entrepreneurial spirit and strict adherence to sound business
practices and procedures will enable it to continue to enjoy its position as a
leading innovator and emerging leader for many years. KENFAB enjoys a
diverse and talented workforce that is firmly committed to the customer and
company’s success for now and beyond.
